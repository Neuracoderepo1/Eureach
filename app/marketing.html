The operational problem

Contacts live in spreadsheets. Calls live in separate tools. Follow-ups disappear between teams.

When outreach is managed across lists, inboxes, messaging apps, notebooks, and disconnected systems, ownership gets unclear and management loses the operational picture. Eureach makes the contact, campaign, assignment, call, outcome, and follow-up one accountable record.

How it works

One operational engine, from contact to outcome.

The same core workflow adapts to the language and operating model of each organization.

01

Capture the record

Name, organization, territory, program, product, or other fields are captured once, using terminology configured for the operation.

02

Prioritize & assign

Set priority, territory, campaign, and ownership so the right coordinator, officer, worker, representative, or agent works the right queue.

03

Reach the right people

Track attempts, calling windows, channels, and segments so teams can improve reach and follow-through.

04

Log the outcome

Successful, unreachable, pending, converted, renewed, enrolled, or follow-up required. Each action becomes part of the operational history.

What's inside

Built as infrastructure, not as an industry-specific app.

Keep the interface simple for frontline agents while giving managers the control, evidence, and configuration needed to run different outreach programs.

Terminology

Speaks your program's language

Configure the record language for Farmer, Patient, Customer, Policyholder, Student, Beneficiary, Respondent, Subscriber, Citizen, or another entity.

Access

Role-based by design

Map platform roles to the operating model: Coordinator, Community Health Worker, Relationship Officer, Field Officer, Admissions Officer, Research Coordinator, Representative, Agent, and more.

Trust

Critical actions are traceable

Record creates, edits, and access decisions with actor, action, and timestamp information so operational changes can be investigated.

Analytics

Reach rate, not guesswork

Turn outreach attempts and outcomes into campaign, territory, team, agent, and program-level operational signals.

Configuration proof
